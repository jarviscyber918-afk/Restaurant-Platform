OUR PHILOSOPHY

TRANSFORMED BY PITMASTERS OVER RAW FIRE.

At CHAR, we stripped away the fuss of traditional dining. We source the finest BMS 11+ Japanese Miyazaki beef and locally raised prime cattle, aging them for 45 days in temperature-controlled pink salt chambers.

Every cut meets the scorching 950°C heat of seasoned Algerian white oak wood. The result is a glass-like caramelized crust, a tender rose core, and pure woodsmoke depth.
